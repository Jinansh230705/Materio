> **Last updated:** 19-04-2025

At **materio** we prioritize your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform.

### 1. Information We Collect

Right now, Materio is a mostly static, client-side platform. However, we may collect limited analytics data to improve your experience:

- **Device & Usage Data** (e.g., browser type, screen size, time spent)  
   Collected via tools like Google Analytics for engagement tracking.
  
- **Cookies** (optional)  
   Used to remember preferences and improve performance. You can opt out in Settings.

- **PDF Interaction Data** (non-personal)  
   Includes scroll activity, zoom level, fullscreen mode usage, and annotation actions—used for analytics and to enhance study tools.

<div class="note">
<strong>Note:</strong> No personal information (like name or email) is currently collected.
</div>


### 2. How We Use Your Data

We use the limited data we collect to:
- Understand which features are most useful
- Improve performance and usability
- Work on new features

We do **not**:
- Sell your data
- Use your data for targeted advertising
- Store or share personally identifiable info (since none is collected right now)


### 3. Cookies

Some essential and analytics cookies may be used to enhance user experience (e.g., Google Analytics). These are **optional** and can be disabled from the **Settings** tab.<br>
For more information about how are cookies used see [cookie policy](/cookies-policy)


### 4. Future Updates

If we rollout newer features in future that might require collection of user data (eg. for Login / Authentication / File Uploads)<br>
At that point, we’ll **update this Privacy Policy** and notify users clearly.


### 5. Your Choices

- You can **opt out of analytics** cookies from the Settings tab.
- In future updates, you'll be able to view, update, or delete your personal data once login features are added.


### Content Disclaimer
All study materials on Materio are shared by students or collected from freely available public sources for educational use only. We do not claim ownership of any content. If you are the original creator of any material and would like it removed or credited, please contact us.
