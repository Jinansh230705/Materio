Materio - A study SaaS that simplifies your CS preparation with verified, crowd-curated notes and fast access to semester-specific resources.

## Features:
- **Modern and Slick UI**:- Extensive work has been put into tailoring the best UI/UX practices.
- **Branch & Semester-Specific Materials**: Access organized content tailored to your syllabus — categorized by subject, semester, and university curriculum.
- **Chat**: A giscus powered discussion area for discussing topics, follow ups, and helping others.
- **Customised Experience**: Toggle theme, Background wallpaper and Select various Reading modes (Paper, Night and E-Ink) for comfortable reading experience.
- **Community Contributions**: Help grow content library by contributing missing resources on Github or mail to support directly.
- **Get Notified**: Remain updated when new material gets uploaded with notifications.
- **The InsightRoom (Beta)**: Get Solutions of assignments, notes and other resources in online mode and offline format with printables.
